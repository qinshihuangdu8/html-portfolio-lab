Wombats are the only known animals in the world that produce cube-shaped poop.

This unusual shape is a result of the unique elasticity and structure of their intestines, which dry out the feces and compress it into sharp angles.

Because wombats have poor eyesight, they stack their droppings on elevated surfaces like rocks and logs to mark their territory.

The flat sides of the cubes prevent the poop from rolling away, ensuring their scent markers stay exactly where they left them.
